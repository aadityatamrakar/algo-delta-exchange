# Desk record and the trader's own

Goal: put the trader's question against how they (get_track_record) and the desk have actually traded.

## The trader's own
- get_track_record (optionally one stock): their expired series with entry timing, short strikes' distance, adjustment days, the largest move from entry, held to expiry or not, and P&L; plus the summary buckets.
- Lead with their figures; bring in the desk's only as the comparison.

## The desk (all staff traders, Jul 2025 – Oct 2026, 424 stock series, 81 index series; P&L gross before charges, approximate)
**How it trades stocks**
- Monthly expiry, opened 20–34 days out (median 27), around the previous expiry.
- Short strangle (40%) or strangle with wings (42%); short strikes ~4.5% out each side (≈0.6 SD).
- Median 9 lots a side, adjusted on 4 days after entry, 9 contracts per series.
- Hedges with options; futures are rare. On a 1.5%+ day the usual response is rolling the untested side toward spot.
- Busiest at the open (09:15–10:00) and the last 30 minutes.
- 81% of series are flat before expiry; median last trade 13 days before it.

**What the results say**
- 74% of series win. Median win ~₹40k; median loss ~₹82k.
- The worst 10% of series lost about as much as all winners made together.
- Those had a median ~10% move from entry while held, and ~13 adjustment days (the rest: ~5.5% and 4).
- Moves under 7% while held: 78–85% won, ~₹78 L made. Moves of 7% or more: 59–69% won, ~₹1.1 Cr lost.
- Series adjusted on 1–7 days won 80–84%; on 8 or more, 60% — and as a group they lost ~₹1.3 Cr.
- Held to expiry with shorts open: 46% won; closed before: 80%.
- IV at entry did not separate winners from losers.

**Index books**: mostly weeklies traded 0–7 days out, often on expiry day; smaller; about 1 in 3 won.

## Reading it
- The edge is many small wins; the risk is one trend month. A book with a 7%+ move from entry or 8+ adjustment days is on the losing profile: say it.
- Never present desk figures as the trader's own. Never name another trader.
